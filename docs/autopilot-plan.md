# Vector Autopilot: the admin sets it up once, Vector runs it

_26 Oct 2026. Direction from Pratik after the first institute demos: the owner should not spend hours in our tool.
They spend a little time on setup; Vector does the work behind the scenes, asks only when a decision is really
theirs, and shows them insights and growth they are happy to see._

## 1. What the owner's week looks like

| When | Owner does | Vector does behind the scenes |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 (about 30 min) | Setup: classes and fees (import), callers (add), WhatsApp (connect), goals (pick), "ask me / do it" choices | Builds fee plans, picks scripts, fills common answers it can, plans the first calling lists |
| Every morning (1–2 min) | Opens Today: reads 3 numbers, taps OK on 0–3 decisions | Overnight: plans the day's calls per caller, creates lists that are due, queues reminders, releases stuck follow-ups |
| During the day (0 min) | Nothing, unless a phone alert says "needs your OK" | Re-orders every caller's list (promises due first, best time to call), balances work between callers, sends reminders, closes paid parents |
| Evening (0–1 min) | Optional: Today's collection sheet | Day closing, receipts sent, tomorrow planned |
| Monday (1–5 min) | Reads the weekly brief on WhatsApp; applies or assigns the Business review advice | Week summary, growth versus last week and last term, 3–4 recommendations from the Advisor (section 6), one "did it work?" result |
| Any time | Asks Vector a question ("Which class owes the most?") | Answers from the institute's own numbers, with a recommended action |

Success measures: owner time under 10 minutes a week after setup; at least 60% of decisions confirmed in one tap;
more fees collected by the due date than last term; admissions confirmed per 100 enquiries going up.

## 2. Three kinds of action (the trust model)

Every behind-the-scenes action belongs to one of three groups. The owner can move an action between "Do it" and
"Ask me" in Settings > Autopilot; money and deletions are always "Ask me".

| Group | Examples | Shown to the owner as |
|---|---|---|
| **Do it quietly** (always safe, reversible, no money) | re-order call lists, give stuck follow-ups to free callers, put promises due today first, pause a list when nobody is left to call, close leads that paid | "Vector did for you" feed (with Undo where possible) |
| **Do it, tell me, let me undo** (default on, owner can switch to Ask) | create the fees calling list 7 days before a due date from the fee plan, assign the best callers, send WhatsApp reminders to parents who agreed, send receipts, adopt a script that won its test with 95%+ certainty | Feed card with a 24-hour Undo |
| **Ask me first** (never automatic) | discounts, changing a fee plan, deleting anything, marketing WhatsApp (paid by Meta's marketing rate), adding a caller seat, contacting people without consent | "Needs your OK" card: one screen, everything filled in, Confirm / Change / Not now |

Guardrails always on: call hours, WhatsApp only with consent and within daily caps, never more than one reminder a day
per parent, Do-not-call is permanent, every automatic action is in the activity log with its reason, one switch pauses
all automation for the institute.

## 3. What the owner sees

**Today (the only screen most owners need)**
1. **Three numbers that matter**, each against the goal: Fees collected this term (₹ and % of due), Admissions
   confirmed this season (count and % of target), Team (calls today, people reached).
2. **Needs your OK** (0–3 cards). Each: what, why (numbers), expected result, one Confirm button.
3. **Vector did for you** (since yesterday): "Created *Term 2 fees – Class 8 State Board* (46 parents, ₹6.2 lakh),
   Ravi and Neha calling 7–9 pm", "Sent 18 payment reminders on WhatsApp", "Gave Amit's 12 waiting parents to Priya".
   Each line has Why and, where possible, Undo.
4. **Growth**: one simple chart, this term versus last term (fees collected by week; admissions by week), plus
   "Vector impact": money paid after a Vector call, follow-ups that would have been missed, hours of calling planned.

5. **Ask Vector** (near the top): one box, "Ask about your calls, fees or team…", with 3 example questions.
6. **Business review · this week**: 3–4 Management and Growth recommendations with Apply / Assign / Later, one
   "Did it work?" line, and "See all advice" to the Advisor page. Details in section 6.

Everything else (lists, scripts, team, reports) stays one tap away but is optional.

**Owner phone**: a WhatsApp message only for "Needs your OK" items marked urgent (for example a list running far
behind before a due date) and the Monday brief (which carries the Business review). Tapping opens the card directly.

## 4. Setup in about 30 minutes (first-run wizard)

1. Institute: name, board(s), classes, working days and hours (defaults: Mon–Sat, 10 am–8 pm).
2. Students and fees: import the Excel (template provided) or pick "Start without fees" (admissions/sales only).
   Vector detects classes, sections, boards and builds a fee plan per class from the sheet; owner confirms.
3. Team: add callers (name + phone); Accounts person if any.
4. Goals: fees collected by due date (default 90%), admissions this season (number), optional sales goal.
5. WhatsApp: connect now or later (reminders and receipts wait until connected).
6. Autopilot choices: three toggles with recommended defaults ("Create calling lists for me", "Send reminders to
   parents who agreed", "Balance work between callers"). Everything else follows section 2.
7. Common answers: 5 short questions (address, landline, website, fee range, discount policy).
8. Business numbers (optional, about 3 min, can be skipped and filled later): what each lead source cost, seats
   per class, last year's numbers, salary date. These switch on the Growth advice (section 6.6).

At the end Vector shows the plan for the next 2 weeks ("3 calling lists will start, about 420 calls, 2 callers")
and the owner taps Start.

## 5. Behind the scenes: the jobs

| Job | When | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Plan fees lists | nightly + when a fee plan or payment changes | For each fee due in the next 7 days with unpaid students, create (or top up) the class's fees list with the right template, callers, hours, target |
| Plan admissions | nightly + when enquiries arrive | Enquiries older than 24 h uncalled go first; season list created when the first enquiries come in |
| Daily call plan per caller | 6 am | Each caller's list ordered: promises due today, callbacks due, best-time-now, then new; daily target set from pace needed |
| Balance work | every 15 min while callers work | Release follow-ups of idle callers (existing rule), move waiting leads to callers with spare capacity in that list |
| Reminders | 9 am and 6 pm | WhatsApp (consent only): day before a promised date, on the due date, after a missed date (once) |
| Close and clean | after each payment / nightly | Close paid parents, pause finished lists, mark wrong numbers, find duplicate students/leads for review |
| Learn | nightly | Caller scores per call type (promises kept, admissions confirmed per 100 picked-up calls), best hours per class, script results, what suggestions get accepted |
| Insights | nightly + Monday | Health line per list, forecast versus goal, 1–2 recommended decisions, weekly brief |

## 6. Advisor and Ask Vector

Vector should act like a business brain for the owner: it notices what is going wrong or could go better, says
what to correct and how to improve, and the owner applies it, hands it to someone, or puts it off. Advice never
runs by itself: Apply opens the same one-screen confirm as "Needs your OK", and money stays "Ask me first"
(section 2).

### 6.1 Three levels of advice

| Level | How often | For | Looks at | Shown in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Operations** | daily | owner, manager | today's lists, missed promises, uncalled enquiries, lists behind pace (the Phase 1 rules) | Today › Needs your OK |
| **Management** | weekly | owner, manager | how callers, hours, scripts and classes perform | Today › Business review, Monday brief, Advisor page |
| **Growth and business** | monthly / per term | owner | where admissions come from and what they cost, next year's admissions, cash flow, this term vs last | Today › Business review, Monday brief, Advisor page, term-end summary |

### 6.2 What the advice looks like (examples)

Numbers are examples for a mid-size institute; real cards use the institute's own data.

| Advice | Level | What we noticed (numbers) | Why it matters | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Caller coaching | Management | Amit's parents keep 38% of their promised dates; the team keeps 61%. In 22 of his last 40 fee calls he skipped the "ask for a date" step. | About ₹1,20,000 a month promised to Amit that arrives late | Assign to [manager]: listen to 3 of Amit's calls and turn on practice mode for the "ask for a date" step; reminder in 3 days |
| Call hours | Management | Parents pick up 29% of calls made 10 am–1 pm and 58% of calls made 4–8 pm (last 4 weeks, 2,300 calls). | Same calls, about 12 caller-hours a week saved | Apply: move fees lists to 4–8 pm |

### 6.3 Every recommendation has the same four parts

1. **What we noticed**: the numbers, the period and how many calls or parents they come from ("Why?" shows the
   full table).
2. **Why it matters**: the effect in ₹, admissions or hours, as an estimate with "about".
3. **Action**, three buttons:
   - **Apply**: opens the pre-filled confirm screen; nothing changes until Confirm.
   - **Assign**: to the manager or a caller, with a due date and a reminder (Today card for them, WhatsApp if
     overdue). The owner sees when it is done.
   - **Later**: snooze 7 days (or "Not useful", which hides this kind of advice for 30 days and teaches Vector).
4. **Did it work?** 14 days after Apply or Assign (term-level advice also at term end), Vector measures the same
   number again and writes one line: "You moved calls to 4–8 pm on 12 Oct: pickups went from 41% to 63%." If it
   did not help, it says so and offers Undo or the next step. Results build a "What worked here" list on the
   Advisor page.

Vector only gives advice when there is enough data (for example at least 200 calls per hour band, 400 calls per
script version, 30 promises per caller), so it does not advise on noise. Coaching advice about a caller is shown
only to the owner and managers, never to other callers.

### 6.4 Ask Vector

A box on Today: "Ask about your calls, fees or team…". The owner types or speaks in English or Hinglish
("Is hafte collection kam kyun hua?"). Vector answers from this institute's data only, shows the numbers it used
and ends with one recommended action and a **Do it** button that opens the usual confirm screen.

| Question | Answer (short, with numbers) | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| Why did collections drop this week? | ₹3.1 lakh this week vs ₹4.6 lakh last week. Most of the drop is Class 10-B (₹1.2 lakh less) and 9 missed promised dates. Ravi was on leave 2 days. | Put the 9 missed parents first tomorrow and share Ravi's list with Priya |
| Who is my best admissions caller? | Neha: 18 admissions per 100 picked-up enquiry calls (team 11), and she calls new enquiries within 3 h. | Give Neha the new Instagram enquiries first |
| Which class owes the most? | Class 10 CBSE: ₹9.8 lakh from 61 students; 10-B alone ₹3.4 lakh. | Start "Term 2 fees – 10-B" with a 2-part plan offer (asks first) |

Rules: if the data cannot answer, Vector says what is missing ("Add what Instagram cost to see cost per
admission") and never guesses; it only sees what the asking person's role may see; it never acts without Confirm.

### 6.5 How it is built

- **Recommendation library**: each advice kind is one SQL rule function (`advice_call_hours(inst)`,
  `advice_caller_coaching(inst)`, …) returning candidates with the numbers, thresholds met, an impact estimate
  (₹ / admissions / hours) and the pre-filled plan. Same `suggestions` table as the Operations rules, with new
  kinds and two new columns: `horizon` (daily / weekly / term) and `level` (operations / management / growth),
  plus `assigned_to`, `remind_at`. Thresholds and on/off per kind live in `advice_rules` (defaults per
  institute size).
- **Outcome tracking**: `advice_outcomes` (suggestion, metric, baseline value and period, applied_at, check_at,
  result value, verdict worked / no change / worse, line shown to the owner). A nightly job fills results when
  `check_at` passes.
- **Business review**: a weekly job (Sunday night) picks the top 3–4 Management and Growth suggestions by impact ×
  confidence, skips anything dismissed in the last 30 days, and adds one due "Did it work?" line. The same set goes
  to Today and to the Monday WhatsApp brief.
- **AI (Haiku)**: only words the cards in English / Hinglish and breaks ties when ranking; it never invents a
  number. Ask Vector uses Haiku with tool calls to read-only, institute-scoped functions (`ask_collections`,
  `ask_dues_by_class`, `ask_callers`, `ask_lead_sources`, `ask_calls_by_hour`, …) that run with the asker's role.
  Every number in an answer must come from a tool result and is shown with its period; the recommended action is
  created as a normal suggestion that needs Confirm. Questions, tools used and answers are logged for review.

### 6.6 Extra setup inputs (optional step 8 of setup)

| Input | Switches on | If skipped |
|---|---|---|
| What each lead source cost this season (fair, Instagram, newspaper, referrals, website) | Lead source ROI | Advice shows enquiries and admissions per source, without ₹ per admission; card asks for the cost |
| Seats per class | Admissions advice by class, "Class 6 is 80% full" | Uses the season target only |
| Last year's numbers (fees collected by month, admissions per class) | Term vs term and re-admission advice from the first week | Starts comparing after one full term in Vector |
| Salary date and monthly salary total | Cash-flow advice | Cash flow shows expected money only |

### 6.7 Success measures

- At least half of Business review advice is applied or assigned within 7 days.
- At least 60% of applied advice shows the measured number improving at the 14-day check.
- Ask Vector answers at least 80% of questions with numbers; weekly spot checks match the reports; zero actions
  without Confirm.
- Owner time still under 10 minutes a week including the review.
- Per institute, term over term: more fees by the due date, more admissions per 100 enquiries, lower cost per
  admission.

## 7. Architecture

- **Database (Supabase Postgres)**
  - `institute_goals` (fees % by due date, admissions target, season dates), `autopilot_settings` (per action
    type: off / ask / auto; global pause), `automation_runs` (job, institute, started, finished, counts, error).
  - `suggestions` (institute, kind, dedupe_key, title, why jsonb (the numbers), impact jsonb, plan jsonb (the
    pre-filled settings), status new/accepted/dismissed/snoozed/expired/auto_done, undo jsonb, expires_at,
    decided_by, decided_at). Unique (institute, dedupe_key) while open.
  - `caller_scores` (seat, call type, period, picked-up calls, good results, promises kept rate, score) refreshed
    nightly; `best_hours` per institute/class.
  - Functions: `autopilot_plan(inst)` (runs the rules, writes suggestions, executes "auto" ones through the
    existing RPCs), `accept_suggestion(id, overrides jsonb)` (one transaction, audit), `undo_suggestion(id)`,
    `dismiss_suggestion(id, snooze)`. Each rule is a small SQL function returning candidate suggestions, so new
    rules are added without touching the rest.
- **Scheduling**: `pg_cron` in Supabase for nightly / morning / 15-minute jobs; plus "plan on event" (payment
  recorded, fee plan applied, lead upload, call saved) and "plan when Today opens and the last plan is over 15
  minutes old". Jobs are idempotent (dedupe keys), so running twice does nothing twice.
- **Messages**: WhatsApp sending reuses the existing whatsapp-send function with caps and consent checks; the owner
  brief reuses weekly-brief.
- **AI**: only to word insights in plain English / Hinglish from numbers the database computed (Haiku, about ₹0.01
  per card), and for Ask Vector through read-only functions (section 6.5). No AI decides money, contacts or
  deletions.
- **Explainability**: every suggestion and automatic action stores the numbers behind it; "Why?" shows them.
- **Safety**: money rules and roles apply unchanged; Accounts never sees calling suggestions; managers see only
  calling ones; the global pause stops all jobs at once.
