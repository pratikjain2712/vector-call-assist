Vector Call Assist ("Vector", "we", "us") is a calling tool for schools, coaching institutes and sales teams ("institutes"). This notice explains what personal data Vector handles, why, where it is stored, who can see it, and how you can use your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and its rules.

1. Short version

  • We store your data in India.
  • We never sell personal data, never show ads and never use it to train AI.
  • We do not record call audio.
  • Each institute can see only its own data. Each caller sees only the leads given to them.
  • Amounts of money are shown only to people the institute allows.
  • We share data only with the few service providers listed in section 6, and only for the purpose stated there.

2. Who is responsible for what

  • Lead and student data (the people an institute calls): the institute decides why and how this data is used, so the institute is the Data Fiduciary. Vector processes it only on the institute's instructions, as its Data Processor. If you were called by an institute, please contact that institute first; we will help them answer you.
  • Account data (admins and callers who sign in) and website visitors: Vector is the Data Fiduciary.

What we do not collect: call audio, contacts, SMS, other entries of your call log, bank, card or UPI details. The phone app reads one call-log row, only for a call placed through Vector, and only if the caller turns that option on. A lead sheet opened on the laptop without a campaign stays in that laptop's browser.

Students and children: institutes call parents and adult enquirers. Where a lead record mentions a student who is a child, the institute is responsible for having the parent's consent as the DPDP Act requires. Vector does not collect data directly from children.

4. Where your data is stored

Vector's database and sign-in service run in India ([PROVIDER], Mumbai region). Backups are kept in the same region. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.

A few services we use to deliver specific features run outside India. They receive only what is listed in section 6.

5. Who can see your data

  • Inside an institute: owners see everything; managers see everything except amounts of money unless an owner allows it; callers see only the leads given to them and the amount due for the lead they are calling. An owner can hide money from everyone.
  • Other institutes: never. Each institute's data is separated by access rules in the database itself.
  • Vector staff: only when needed to run the service, fix a problem you report, or meet a legal duty. Staff access is limited and logged.

7. How long we keep data

  • While the institute's account is active, for as long as the institute needs it. Institutes can delete campaigns and leads.
  • When an institute closes its account, we delete its data within [30] days, and it leaves our backups within [7] more days. We can give the institute a copy first.
  • We keep billing records and the activity log for as long as the law requires.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask to access, correct, complete or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, nominate someone to act for you, and raise a grievance. For data an institute collected about you, contact the institute first; we will help them respond. You can also write to our Grievance Officer below. We reply within [the period set by the DPDP Rules]. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Security and breaches

Access to data is controlled by the database itself, not only by the app. Admin actions and money exports are logged. If a personal data breach happens, we will inform the affected institutes and the Data Protection Board of India, and help institutes inform the people affected, as the law requires.

10. Cookies and tracking

The website uses no advertising or analytics trackers. Your browser stores your sign-in session and a few settings (such as text size) so the app works. [The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.]

We will post any change here with a new date, and tell institute owners by email before a change that affects how their data is used.
