Fees collection

Insights

Know what to change before the month ends.

Vector turns your calls into a few clear answers: where you will land, what closes the gap, and which line in the script loses people.

  • Forecast with a range. Fees collected or seats filled by the end date, against your target. Move the what-if sliders: add a caller, call more in the evening.
  • Best time to call. Pickup rate by day and hour, so callers ring when parents actually answer.
  • Promises kept and caller coaching. Which promises turn into payments, and which caller skips which step, with practice mode to fix it.
  • Script tests and a weekly brief. Run two versions side by side and keep the winner. Owners can get a four-line brief every Monday.

AI script maker

A ready script in about a minute.

No blank page. Fill a short form and get a draft in simple words, with steps, pushback answers and endings linked to results.

  1. Tell it about the call. Call type, who you call, what the call is for, the facts the caller can use, and what never to say.
  2. It checks itself. The AI uses only your facts. Any other number or date gets a "Check this" mark, and the script cannot be published until you clear it.
  3. You approve it. Change any line, then publish. Callers only ever see scripts you have published.

    Trust and privacy

    Parents' numbers and your fee data stay yours.

    You are trusting us with parents' phone numbers and money owed to you. Here is exactly how we look after it.

    • No call recording

      Vector never records call audio. It saves the result, the time and the script step, nothing more.

    • Your data, walled off

      Each institute's data is kept apart inside the database itself, so one institute can never read another's.

    • Callers see only their leads

      A caller sees the leads given to them, and the amount due for the lead on the call. Never totals, never other lists.

    • Money only for the right people

      Owner, manager and caller roles. Turn "can see money" on per person, or hide money everywhere so reports show counts only.

    • Every sensitive step logged

      An activity log shows who changed roles, campaigns, leads and scripts, and every export that included money.

    • Never sold, no ads

      We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it to train AI models. We store amounts and dates, never bank, card or UPI details.

    Built for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Read the privacy notice and the terms of service.

    Questions

    Straight answers.

    Is this AI calling?

    No. Vector is for human callers. A person dials, talks and chooses the result. Vector puts the script, the pushback answers and the callbacks in front of them. AI only helps you write scripts.

    Do you record calls?

    No. Vector does not record call audio.

    Does it work for fees collection and admissions?

    Yes, those are what Vector is built for. Each call type has its own one-tap results: fees (Paid, Will pay on date with the amount, Paid part, Says already paid), admissions (Visit booked, Form sent, Admission confirmed), sales (Interested, Joined, Will think), and more, such as PTM reminders and renewals.

    What does each caller need?

    An Android phone with a working SIM and calling credit, a laptop with Chrome or Edge, and the same Wi-Fi for both. Setup takes about 20 minutes per caller.

    Can callers use an iPhone?

    Not yet. The phone app runs on Android. On the laptop, use Chrome or Edge.

    How do my callers get the phone app?

    After you start the trial, the Vector team sends you the Android app and helps you set up the first phone. Each caller then installs it, scans the code on their laptop once, and is ready to call.

    What about TRAI rules and DND?

    Calls go out from your callers' own numbers, so your institute and your callers must follow TRAI's rules for calling, including the Do Not Disturb register and calling hours. Vector helps: when a person asks not to be called, the caller taps "Do not call again", Vector records it and stops offering that lead. You can also set the hours when calls may be made.

    What leaves the laptop?

    Call outcomes, timings and seat details, so the admin can see results. Leads you upload to a campaign are stored in your Vector account so they can be shared between callers; each caller sees only the leads given to them. A lead sheet you open on the laptop without a campaign stays on that laptop.

    Where is my data stored?

    In a managed cloud database, with each institute's data kept apart. The privacy notice says exactly where it is stored today and what changes before launch.

    How much do WhatsApp messages cost?

    WhatsApp follow-ups and payment reminders go from your institute's own business number. Meta charges for these messages at its own rates, and Settings shows this month's count and estimated cost.

    Is GST extra?

    Yes. All prices are in rupees and GST at 18% is added to your invoice.

    Can I cancel anytime?

    Yes. Cancel at any time and your plan runs until the end of the period you have paid for. The free trial ends on its own after 5 days and nothing is charged.

    Do I need a card for the trial?

    No. The trial is 5 days with up to 5 seats and needs no payment.
