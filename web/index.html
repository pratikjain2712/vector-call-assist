Vector 2.0 call guide · first-time setup

Connect your phone

Calls go out from your phone's SIM, and the phone reports when each call connects and ends. Pair once; this browser remembers the phone.

  1. On the phone, open Vector Companion and tap Show pairing QR. Phone and laptop must be on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Here, scan the QR code with the laptop camera, or type in the address and code shown under it.

Hold the phone's QR code up to the camera. It pairs as soon as it reads the code.
