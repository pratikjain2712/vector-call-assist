    Analytics

    A CRM records the result. Vector shows where the call was lost.

    A traditional CRM tells you a lead said no. Vector shows where in the call it was lost, why, and what to change. The team sees it the same day, and the script improves with every version.

    Which step loses the call

    Drop-off by script step, so you change the one line that costs the most, not the whole script.

    Why the call was lost

    A rep taps one reason on a lost call: price, timing, already enrolled, no need or wrong person. It takes a second and it sharpens the next call.

    The script gets better with each version

    Each version is compared with the one before. The system suggests what to change, the admin approves it, and the team sees what changed.

    How it works

    From invite to first call in four steps.

    1. 1

      Invite

      Add each rep by email. Every invite takes one seat, and the rep signs in with an emailed link.

    2. 2

      Pair

      Scan the code on the laptop from the Android app. The phone and laptop pair to that seat over local Wi-Fi.

    3. 3

      Work the list

      The laptop shows the next lead and the script. Each Dial starts the call on the rep's SIM through the paired phone.

    4. 4

      Results

      Each outcome is saved on the laptop first, then synced per seat. Admins see the team's progress live.

    Questions

    Straight answers.

    Is this AI calling?

    No. Vector is for human callers. A person dials, talks and chooses the outcome. Vector puts the script, the objection answers and the callbacks in front of them.

    Can reps use an iPhone?

    Not yet. The phone app runs on Android. On the laptop, use Chrome or Edge.

    How do my reps get the phone app?

    After you start the trial, the Vector team sends you the Android app and helps you set up the first phone. Each rep then installs it, scans the code on their laptop once, and is ready to call.

    What does each rep need?

    An Android phone with a working SIM and calling credit, a laptop with Chrome or Edge, and the same Wi-Fi for both. Setup takes about 20 minutes per rep.

    Do you record calls?

    No. Vector does not record call audio.

    What leaves the laptop?

    Call outcomes, timings and seat details, so the admin can see results. Leads you upload to a campaign are stored in your Vector account so they can be shared between callers; each caller sees only the leads given to them. A lead sheet you open on the laptop without a campaign stays on that laptop.

    Do I need a card for the trial?

    No. The trial is 5 days with up to 5 seats and needs no payment.
