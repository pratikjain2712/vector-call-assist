The product

The phone dials. The laptop thinks.

The cloud gets only what an admin needs to run the team: outcomes, timings and targets. Move your pointer over a card to see it tilt.

01 · PHONE

Calls from the rep's own SIM

The Android app dials on the phone's own line. Each rep keeps their number. Nothing routes through a shared calling service.

02 · LAPTOP

The script, one line at a time

The laptop shows the published script for the current step, with the next line ready. Admins publish a new version and reps get it on their next sign-in.

03 · HELP

Objection answers in reach

Common objections sit next to the script, so the rep is never stuck on a question they have heard a hundred times.

04 · REMINDERS

Callbacks that ring back

Set a callback on the laptop and the phone reminds the rep at the time they chose, with the lead's name on screen.

05 · SYNC

Outcomes that never go missing

Each outcome is saved on the phone first and sent when the connection returns. Each one is stored once, however many retries it takes.

06 · ADMIN

The team on one page

Team activity, results by outcome, per-rep targets, the weekly summary and supervisor notes, all from the admin page.

    How it works

    From invite to first call in four steps.

    Admin

    Invite each rep by email.

    Every invite takes one seat. The rep signs in with an emailed link and the seat is attached to their login. Nobody joins without an invite.

    Rep, once

    Scan the code. Pair once.

    Open the Android app and scan the code on the laptop. The phone and laptop pair to that seat over your local Wi-Fi, and only that seat's phone and laptop can use it.

    Rep, every day

    Work the list from the laptop.

    The lead sheet opens on the rep's device. The phone dials the number on the current row. The laptop shows the script for each step, with the objection answers close by.

    Admin, live

    Results arrive as the calls happen.

    Each outcome is saved on the phone, then sent to the cloud. If the Wi-Fi drops mid-day, the queue waits and uploads when the connection is back. Admins see every seat's progress.

    Questions

    Straight answers.

    Is this AI calling?

    No. Vector is for human callers. A person dials, talks and chooses the outcome. Vector puts the script, the objection answers and the callbacks in front of them.

    Can reps use an iPhone?

    Not yet. The phone app runs on Android. Laptops use any modern browser.

    Do you record calls?

    No. Vector does not record call audio.

    What leaves the rep's device?

    Call outcomes, timings and seat details, so the admin can see results. Lead names and phone numbers stay on the rep's device.

    Can one person use two laptops?

    No. A seat is paired with one phone and one laptop. A second laptop needs its own seat.

    Do I need a card for the trial?

    No. The trial is 14 days with up to 5 seats and needs no payment. Paid plans open later.
