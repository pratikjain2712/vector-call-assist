The product

Built for people who make calls all day.

The phone does the dialling. The laptop does the thinking. The cloud gets only what the admin needs to run the team: outcomes, timings and targets.

Phone

Calls from the rep's own SIM

The Android app dials on the phone's own line. Each rep keeps their number. You do not need a shared calling service.

Laptop

The script, one line at a time

The laptop shows the published script for the current step, with the next line ready. Admins publish a new version; reps get it on their next sign-in.

Help

Objection answers in reach

Common objections sit next to the script, so the rep is never stuck on a question they have heard a hundred times.

Reminders

Callbacks that ring back

Set a callback on the laptop and the phone reminds the rep at the time they chose, with the lead's name on the screen.

Sync

Outcomes that never go missing

Each outcome is saved on the phone first and sent when the connection returns. Each one is stored once, however many retries it takes.

Admin

The team on one page

Team activity, results by outcome, per-rep targets, weekly summary and supervisor notes, all from the admin page.

How it works

From invite to first call in four steps.

Admin

Invite each rep by email.

Every invite takes one seat. The rep signs in with an emailed link and the seat is attached to their login. Nobody can join without an invite.

Rep, once

Scan the code. Pair once.

Open the Android app and scan the code on the laptop. The phone and laptop pair to that seat over your local Wi-Fi, and only the seat's own phone and laptop can use it.

Rep, every day

Work the list from the laptop.

The lead sheet opens on the rep's device. The phone dials the number on the current row. The laptop shows the script for each step and the objection answers close by.

Admin, live

Results arrive as the calls happen.

Each outcome is saved on the phone, then sent to the cloud. If the Wi-Fi drops mid-day, the queue waits and uploads when the connection is back. Admins see every seat's progress.

Seats

One seat is one person.

You buy seats, not devices or sites. A seat is one person, one phone and one laptop. It can change hands when someone leaves.

1person per seat

The seat is tied to one login and one rep's work.

1phone per seat

One Android phone is paired to the seat at a time.

1laptop per seat

One laptop is paired to the seat. A second laptop needs its own seat.

↺seat can move

An admin reassigns a seat to someone new. The old person's access ends at their next sign-in.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is this AI calling?

No. Vector is for human callers. A person dials, talks and chooses the outcome. Vector puts the script, the objection answers and the callbacks in front of them.

Can reps use an iPhone?

Not yet. The phone app runs on Android. Laptops use any modern browser.

Do you record calls?

No. Vector does not record call audio.

What leaves the rep's device?

Call outcomes, timings and seat details, so the admin can see results. Lead names and phone numbers stay on the rep's device.

Can one person use two laptops?

No. A seat is paired with one phone and one laptop. A second laptop needs its own seat.

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. The trial is 14 days with up to 5 seats and needs no payment. Paid plans open later.
